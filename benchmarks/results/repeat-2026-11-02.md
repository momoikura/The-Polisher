# Fixing the stuck runs, 2026-11-02

Most unproven runs on [2026-11-01](final-2026-11-01.md) weren't hitting hard problems. They were losing their time to two mechanical failures:

- **Hung tests.** A test the model wrote could hang, and the sandbox waited out its full 300 seconds with no useful feedback.
- **Model timeouts.** A repair call could hit the 300-second limit, and the whole round was lost. pyparsing#693 and astroid#3255 each lost two rounds this way.

So a test now stops at 60 seconds and fails with a message, related tests stop at 120 seconds with a note about what probably hung, and CodePolish thinks for less after a timeout. Then I ran the same 16 issues twice ([list 1](../repeat1-2026-11-02.json), [list 2](../repeat2-2026-11-02.json), [results 1](repeat1-2026-11-02.jsonl), [results 2](repeat2-2026-11-02.jsonl)).

## Result

| | 2026-11-01 | Repeat 1 | Repeat 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Proven | 11 of 16 | 13 of 16 | 14 of 16 |
| Proven, with no new failures | 10 | **13** | **14** |
| Clean or verified | 9 | 10 | 11 |
| First draft alone proves the fix | 5 | 6 | 8 |
| Median time | 8.6 min | 6.7 min | 9.3 min |
| Total model cost | $0.43 | $0.48 | $0.44 |

**More fixes get proven, and it isn't faster.** The median time went down in one repeat and up in the other, so I don't claim a speed gain. The first-draft count moved between 5 and 8 with no change to how drafts are written, which shows how much a single run varies. That's why this is three runs, and why I quote the lower repeat, 13 of 16.

## Method

Each issue ran once per list, three at a time, with a $0.50 cap, on Muse Spark 1.3 Contributor, without Muse Wonder. The issues are the same ones chosen before the first run. Every result is kept, failures included.
